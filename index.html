Who We Are

About Catalyst

Catalyst is a youth-driven initiative that equips young people to learn, create, collaborate, and address real-world challenges. We bring together ambitious students, emerging innovators, mentors, speakers, and professionals through purposeful experiences that build both capability and confidence.

Our work spans hackathons, competitions, talks, workshops, mentorship, and community-led challenges. Each initiative is designed to move participants beyond passive inspiration: they investigate ideas, strengthen practical skills, form valuable relationships, and translate their potential into action.

Catalyst is intentionally multidisciplinary. Whether a participant is interested in technology, medicine, science, entrepreneurship, sustainability, or social impact, they enter a community united by curiosity, initiative, and a commitment to positive change.

Our Purpose

To create the conditions in which young people can turn curiosity into capability, ideas into solutions, and solutions into lasting impact.

Vision

A generation of confident, innovative young people equipped to shape a better future.

Mission

To provide inclusive learning experiences, meaningful connections, and practical opportunities that enable young people to explore, collaborate, build, and lead.

What We Do

From First Exposure to Confident Action

Our programs follow the progression EXPLORE → LEARN → BUILD → IMPACT across six core pillars:

Hackathons & Competitions

Team-based experiences where participants define problems, test ideas, and develop solutions under focused conditions.

Talks & Speaker Sessions

Direct access to experts and professionals who share practical knowledge, career insight, and lessons from experience.

Workshops

Hands-on sessions that introduce useful tools, strengthen technical and creative skills, and make unfamiliar fields accessible.

Mentorship

Supportive relationships that help participants refine ideas, navigate challenges, and make informed decisions about their growth.

Innovation Challenges

Structured prompts that develop critical thinking, creativity, resourcefulness, and a solution-oriented mindset.

Community Initiatives

Ongoing spaces for peer learning, collaboration, knowledge exchange, and partnerships that extend beyond a single event.

The Impact We Seek

Why Catalyst
